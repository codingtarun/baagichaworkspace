### 3. PURPOSE OF API ACCESS

Baagicha is an agricultural technology platform designed to assist orchard farmers in the Himalayan regions of **Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Jammu & Kashmir**. The platform provides:

- Real-time weather information and 7-day forecasts
- Spray window optimization advisories
