Mockup: a second colour

Today the app is green for everything: buttons, links, icons, pills, cards. Gold appears only in small pills and means both "do now" and "warning". The idea: keep green for actions (buttons, links, the selected tab), keep red and gold for warnings, and add one accent for what is new or due now: the "Do now" pill, new stories, the calendar tile, the record-work icons. Same Home, same data; only the accent changes. Pick one (or "keep as now").

Today (no accent)
Gold "Do now" looks like the frost and weather warnings; everything else is green.
A · Saffron (warm)
Warm like a hill sunrise or a ripe apricot; strong next to green. Close to gold, so warnings must stay clearly yellow.
B · Himalayan sky (cool)
Fresh and calm, never confused with a warning; ties in with the weather screen's sky header. Most readable (8.4 : 1).
C · Apple blossom (fruit)
The orchard's own pink: friendly and unlike any other farm app. Pink must stay away from red, so alerts keep their icon.

The rules that come with it

Green = do somethingButtons, links, the selected tab, "done" and "good".
Accent = new or due now"Do now", new stories, the calendar, record-work icons, the stage marker. Fill colour only for icons, dots and rings; text in the darker accent.
Gold and red = carefulGold for warnings (frost, rain soon, proposed ban), red for danger and errors. Never used for "do now" any more.
ContrastEvery accent text colour reads at 5.7 : 1 or better on white, the cream page and its soft background (rule: 4.5).
